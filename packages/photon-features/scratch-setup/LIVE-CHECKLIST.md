7. Re-submit the same completed final request. It must not send twice. Induce a duplicate
   webhook in the authorized test; it must not create duplicate successful work.
8. Kill the runtime child (not unrelated processes). Confirm exactly one replacement,
   same state and safe stale-owner recovery. Send a *fresh* message afterward. Do not
   claim this proves recovery of every upstream message sent during downtime.
9. End the bot turn and close its initiating terminal. Verify listening continues.
   Test cold boot/resume and at least one overnight idle interval on the actual host.
   A suspended VM is a platform boundary, not an idle Grok turn.
10. Intentional stop must remain stopped. Verify service registration is also stopped
    for cutover. Preserve all queued, unknown and delivered effects during upgrade or
    rollback. Do not restore a stale queue snapshot or clear the database to recover.
